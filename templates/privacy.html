Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

We collect information you directly provide when using Spendly, including your email address, username, password hash, and expense records (such as transaction amounts, categories, dates, and optional notes).

How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to provide, maintain, and improve the core expense tracking services of Spendly. We use your financial records solely to display summaries, analytics, and category breakdowns for your personal account.

Data Storage

We implement appropriate technical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Your data is stored securely in encrypted databases.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or expense history to third parties. We do not integrate with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.app.
